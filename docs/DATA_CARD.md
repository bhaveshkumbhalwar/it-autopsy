## Split handling

- The upstream train/test split is preserved exactly and is never reshuffled.
- `data/processed/test_untouched.csv` is the test set. It is never modified.
- `data/processed/train_leakage_reduced.csv` removes, from **training only**,
  texts that also occur in test and texts with conflicting labels.
- Both training variants are evaluated and reported; the untouched official
  split is reported alongside the leakage-reduced one.
- The EOL class has 2 unique training texts and 2 unique test texts; all 2 training texts also occur in test. Strict overlap removal therefore leaves **zero EOL training rows**. The classifier uses a deterministic phrase rule for explicit EOL wording and reports the base model separately.

## Known limitations

- Duplicate tickets mean row-level accuracy over-weights repeated wording.
- There is train/test leakage in the upstream split (see numbers above).
- The EOL class is template-identical across splits and disappears under
  strict leakage removal; no zero-shot EOL generalisation is claimed.
- No language labels exist; per-language performance cannot be claimed.
- No OS field and no resolution outcome: the data supports triage only.
- Categories are the source authors' seven labels, not our invention.
